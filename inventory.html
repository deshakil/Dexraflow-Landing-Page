    FAQ

    Short answers.

    Do you see my workflows?

    No. Everything you type stays in your browser unless you download it.

    What counts as a workflow?

    Anything that moves a lead or deal automatically: form syncs, routing rules, enrichment, Zaps, sequences, AI agents writing to your CRM.

    Why 'within a day'?

    Most silent breaks are found weeks later, by a rep or a customer. A day is the bar worth aiming for.

    What's Dexraflow?
