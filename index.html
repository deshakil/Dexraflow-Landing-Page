How it works

Four things happen, and you don't touch any of them.

  1. Your phone rings. Nobody can get it.

    Missed call or web form, it comes to us within a minute.

  2. Second Ring answers

    Scripts written for heating and cooling work. It says it's an assistant, asks what's wrong, and offers a person.

  3. The job lands on your calendar

    Straight into Jobber or Housecall Pro, with the caller's details and the problem.

  4. Every Monday, a report

    Jobs booked, money recovered, emergencies passed on, and whether the desk stayed up. A person checks every call the morning after.

Questions owners ask

Run by Sayyad Shakil. I build and check every setup myself. Email me at shakil@dexraflow.in.

Is this a robot answering my phone?

Yes, and it says so. The assistant tells callers it's an assistant for your shop. It asks the few questions needed to book a visit, and every caller is offered a transfer to a person or a text to you. The next morning, a person on our team reads the calls and fixes anything it got wrong.

Does it work with Jobber or Housecall Pro?

Yes. Visits are booked straight into your Jobber or Housecall Pro calendar, so your schedule stays in one place. If you use something else, say so on the audit form and we'll tell you honestly whether we can connect to it.

What happens in an emergency?

The assistant answers 24/7. No-heat emergencies go straight to your own on-call tech, and if nobody picks up, the tech gets a text with the caller's name, number and the problem. Our team reviews every call the next morning.

What if it goes down?

Calls fall back to your normal voicemail and you get an instant text. We tell you before you find out yourself, and any downtime shows on your weekly report.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. It runs month to month after the free audit, and you can stop at any time.

Where are you based?

We're a small team in Hyderabad, India, working with heating and cooling shops in the US, starting with Columbus, Ohio. You'll deal with the same people throughout, and you can email the founder directly at shakil@dexraflow.in.
