    FAQ

    Frequently askedquestions.

    Do I have to replace any of my tools?

    No. Dexraflow connects to the CRM and GTM tools you already use, read-only by default, and catches what slips between them. Nothing is migrated.

    Does Dexraflow email or contact my prospects?

    Never. It tells the right rep in Slack, with the reason and a deadline. People act; Dexraflow informs, allows, holds or logs.

    What does the 14-day audit involve?

    You connect your tools read-only. We look back 90 days and show you the high-intent accounts nobody worked, leads routed to the wrong owner or no owner, and workflows that failed silently, with what each one was worth. If we don't find at least $10K of missed pipeline, you don't pay.

    Which tools does it work with?

    CRMs like HubSpot, Salesforce and Attio; enrichment and data from Clay, Apollo and ZoomInfo; sequencing in Outreach and Salesloft; calls in Gong and Zoom; support in Intercom and Zendesk; plus Slack, Gmail, Google Calendar, Calendly, LinkedIn, Zapier and n8n. More on request. Product names identify the tools we connect to; trademarks belong to their owners.

    What is the Dexraflow MCP?

    An early-access way for AI agents to ask Dexraflow before they act in your CRM. It checks every tool, applies your rules, then answers allow, hold or wait, and logs the decision. It works with any client that supports remote MCP.

    Who is this for?

    Heads of RevOps at B2B SaaS companies running a CRM plus five or more GTM tools. We're working with a small group of early-access teams.
