Last updated 23 November 2026

This page explains what Second Ring collects when you use this website, what we do with it, and how to have it removed. We've kept it short on purpose.

Who we are

Second Ring is a service by Dexraflow, run by Sayyad Shakil, 1-25-104, Santoshpuri Colony, Sitarampuram, Bowenpally, Tirumalagiri, Hyderabad 500011, India. Email: shakil@dexraflow.in.

What we collect

Only what you type into the audit request form: your name, your shop's name, your email address, your phone number and which booking tool you use. We don't collect anything else, and we don't use cookies or analytics on this site.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to you and set up the free missed-call audit you asked for.
  • To run the audit and, if you go ahead, the booking desk service.

We don't sell your details, and we don't add you to marketing lists. If you ask for an audit and decide not to continue, we delete your details when you tell us, or after a reasonable time if we don't hear back.

Where it goes

Form submissions are stored in a private database we run on Supabase, hosted in the United States, which only we can read. If the form can't send, it offers you a prefilled email instead, which goes directly from your mail app to ours.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete anything we hold about you at any time. Email shakil@dexraflow.in and we'll do it.

Changes

If we change this page, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use details you've already given us, we'll email you first.
