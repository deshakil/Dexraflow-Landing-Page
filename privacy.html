This page explains what Second Ring collects when you use this website, what we do with it, and how to have it removed. We've kept it short on purpose.

Who we are

Second Ring is a service by Dexraflow, run by Sayyad Shakil, 1-25-104, Santoshpuri Colony, Sitarampuram, Bowenpally, Tirumalagiri, Hyderabad 500011, India. Email: shakil@dexraflow.in.

What the form collects

Only what you type into the audit request form: your name, your shop's name, your email address, your phone number if you choose to give it, which booking tool you use and how many technicians you have. We don't use cookies or analytics on this site.

What we use it for

  • To reply to you and set up the free missed-call audit you asked for.
  • To run the audit and, if you go ahead, the booking desk service.

We don't sell your details, and we don't add you to marketing lists. Submissions are stored in a private database we run on Supabase, hosted in the United States, which only we can read.

Deleting your details

Email shakil@dexraflow.in and we'll delete anything we hold about you, usually the same day. If you ask for an audit and decide not to continue, we delete your details when you tell us, or after a reasonable time if we don't hear back.

Call recordings for clients

If your shop becomes a client, calls the assistant handles are recorded so that a person can review them the next morning. The assistant says so at the start of every call. How those recordings are stored, for how long, and who can hear them is set out in the client agreement, not in this website policy.

Changes

If we change this page, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use details you've already given us, we'll email you first.
